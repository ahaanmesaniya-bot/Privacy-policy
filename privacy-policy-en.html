3. Information We Do Not Collect

R7 Play does not require you to create an account. We do not ask for, collect or use:

The App saves the following data locally on your device only:

This data is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time using the "Clear" options in Settings, by clearing the App's storage in your phone settings, or by uninstalling the App.

This is how all internet requests work. We do not use this information to identify you.

Advertising partners may also use their own cookies or similar technologies. You can clear cookies and website data through your device settings, but some game progress may be lost if you do so.

You can control advertising on your device at any time:

We do not sell your personal information.
